5 Signs Your Suspension Needs Expert Service or Rebuild

Suspension doesn't last forever. Worn components affect handling, comfort, and safety. Know the warning signs for your next ride.

  • Excessive bouncing and bumps or landings
  • Bottoming out on small or moderate impacts
  • Leaking fork or shock oil
  • Uneven or unstable handling in corners
  • Harsh ride quality or increased rider fatigue
