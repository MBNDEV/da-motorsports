  • How long does suspension tuning take?

    Some setups can be done the same day while others may take up to 5 days to complete. We always strive to provide the quickest turnaround in the industry.

  • What's included in a suspension rebuild?

    A complete rebuild includes a full teardown of the forks and shock. We clean everything, and inspect for any worn components. We replace worn bushings and hard parts if needed, reassemble with new seals, oil and nitrogen. We offer this service to customers who drop off the entire motorcycle, or just the forks and shock.

  • Do you tune for different rider weights and abilities?

    Yes, our services are custom, not generic. They're designed specifically for the individual rider.

  • What's the difference between factory and standard suspension?

    Factory Level Racing suspension is designed for professional riders who need precise, track-specific tuning based on terrain, speed, and riding style. It is not designed for the average rider. Generic "kit" setups often used for novices can feel harsh and poorly balanced. We tailor each suspension to match the rider's true ability for maximum control and performance.

  • How do I know if I need a re-valve or service?

    A service will keep your suspension operating as intended with fresh oil and new seals. This is recommended every 20-40 hours depending on your riding style. A re-valve is different. It's a specific tuning process that provides you with suspension that works best for YOU. The result is a much safer, comfortable and predictable motorcycle.
